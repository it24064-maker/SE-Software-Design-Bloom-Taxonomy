### Course Outcomes (COs) Mapping Reference
* **CO1:** Understand the fundamental concepts, principles, and modularity of software design.
* **CO2:** Evaluate software design quality and complexity using standard Object-Oriented (CK) metrics.
* **CO3:** Analyze and compare different software architectural styles and Enterprise Application Architecture (EAA) patterns.
* **CO4:** Explain the intent, structure, and trade-offs of Gang of Four (GoF) object-oriented design patterns.
* **CO5:** Apply fundamental User Interface (UI) design principles, modalities, and presentation guidelines.

### Question Paper (Total Marks: 40)

#### **Question 1: Design Fundamentals & Quality Metrics [Marks: 10]**
* **(a)** Define Software Design. Distinguish between *Architectural Design* and *Detailed Design* in the software design process. **[Marks: 4] [Bloom's: Knowledge & Understanding] [CO1]**
* **(b)** What is meant by *Coupling* and *Cohesion* in software modularity? Arrange the types of Coupling from lowest (best) to highest (worst). **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Knowledge & Understanding] [CO1]**
* **(c)** Explain the Chidamber & Kemerer (CK) metrics: **CBO** (Coupling Between Object classes) and **LCOM** (Lack of Cohesion of Methods). Why is a lower LCOM value desirable in object-oriented design? **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO2]**

---

#### **Question 2: Software Architecture & EAA Patterns [Marks: 10]**
* **(a)** Define *Software Architecture*. Briefly describe the **Model-View-Controller (MVC)** architectural style with its three distinct roles. **[Marks: 4] [Bloom's: Knowledge & Understanding] [CO3]**
* **(b)** Differentiate between **Pipes and Filters** and **Event-Based** architectural styles with suitable examples. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO3]**
* **(c)** In Martin Fowler's Enterprise Application Architecture (EAA), compare **Single Table Inheritance** and **Class Table Inheritance** for mapping an inheritance hierarchy to a relational database, highlighting one advantage and one disadvantage of each. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO3]**

---

#### **Question 3: Gang of Four (GoF) Design Patterns [Marks: 10]**
* **(a)** List the three classifications of GoF design patterns with two examples for each category. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Knowledge] [CO4]**
* **(b)** Explain the intent and participants (structure) of the **Adapter** design pattern. How does an *Object Adapter* differ from a *Class Adapter*? **[Marks: 4] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO4]**
* **(c)** Describe how the **Observer** pattern maintains consistency between a *Subject* and multiple *Observers* without tight coupling. Mention one liability of this pattern. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO4]**

---

#### **Question 4: Presentation Logic & User Interface (UI) Design [Marks: 10]**
* **(a)** Differentiate between **Page Controller** and **Front Controller** presentation logic patterns in web applications. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO3]**
* **(b)** Identify and briefly explain any four fundamental **UI Design Principles** (e.g., Learnability, Consistency, Minimal Surprise, User Familiarity). **[Marks: 4] [Bloom's: Knowledge & Understanding] [CO5]**
* **(c)** Explain three guidelines for effective **UI Information Presentation** using colors, particularly considering users with color blindness or visual deficiencies. **[Marks: 3] [Bloom's: Understanding] [CO5]**
